Privacy Policy

Last updated: this page is a starter template — review with qualified counsel before publishing.

This website is operated by Go Scale Me ("we", "us"). It provides service information and contact and podcast studio quote request forms. It does not operate user accounts or advertising tracking.

What this website collects

This website does not use analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies. When you submit a form, its details are transmitted to our server, validated, and passed to our transactional email provider, Resend, for delivery to rex@goscale.me. The contact form includes your name, reply email, brand, goals, request type and optional preferred meeting time and time zone. Do not include sensitive personal information.

When a request is accepted for delivery, we temporarily save its contents in a private server-side database queue, so a brief email-provider outage does not lose it. We retry for up to 6 hours, with no more than 8 attempts. Contents are deleted after provider acceptance or when delivery attempts end; expired contents are removed on the next cleanup pass while the server and database are available. If the service is offline, cleanup resumes when it restarts. A keyed, non-readable fingerprint and delivery status remain for up to 24 hours to prevent duplicate submissions, then are removed on cleanup. Hosting backups may retain earlier copies under the hosting provider's retention policy. We do not use this queue as a contact database.

If saving fails, your entries stay only in the current browser page for correction or retry and may be lost if you leave or reload. Our server also uses temporary, in-memory request counters, including hashed network and email identifiers, to limit abuse. These counters expire within 10 minutes. Application logs record request status and general delivery errors, not form contents or email credentials.

Like any website, the hosting provider that serves these pages may process standard technical data (such as IP address and request logs) to deliver and secure the site.

Enquiries and email correspondence

We use the information you submit or email directly to reply, prepare quotes, discuss a potential working relationship and arrange meetings. We don't sell it. Resend and our mailbox provider process messages for transmission and delivery under their own terms and retention policies; processing may take place outside your country. We may keep correspondence in our mailbox for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes and our ordinary business records. “Saved for delivery” means the request is queued, not yet confirmed as sent. Provider acceptance does not guarantee inbox delivery, that a person has read it or that a booking is confirmed.

Meeting requests

A proposed meeting time is only a request. No calendar booking is created by this website; we'll confirm by replying.

The podcast studio estimate is calculated in your browser. When you send a quote request, the selected package, hours, add-ons, indicative USD estimate, preferred date and UAE location, name or brand, reply email and show notes are sent through the same server and email delivery process. This does not reserve a session, confirm availability or take payment.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete correspondence you've sent by emailing rex@goscale.me. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Third-party links

This site may load fonts from Google Fonts and may link to external platforms such as TikTok, which have their own privacy practices.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The current version will always live on this page.
